Survey patterns shown: progressive single-section disclosure, 5-point Likert scales, in-context guidance, progress indication and character-limited reflection fields.

Design notes

Why the experience is built this way — for your review, Ian.

Progressive disclosure

One section at a time with a clear progress bar (Typeform / Qualtrics pattern). Lowers cognitive load and lifts completion — important for a reflective, emotionally weighty task.

5-point Likert scale

The Real Me uses a balanced 1–5 scale with labelled end-anchors, the industry standard for self-report enjoyment and agreement.

Guidance in context

Each strengths block opens with its "enjoyment check" so the guidance is read at the moment of answering, not buried in instructions.

Continuum capture

The Wellbeing Profile keeps your Thriving / Struggling / Suffering model, shown as three clear, colour-coded choices per life area.

Constrained reflection

Deep Dive answers use a 200-character limit with a live counter and "principle-level" microcopy — keeping responses sharp and usable for the UVP.

Sequence & save

Wellbeing → Real Me → Deep Dive, mirroring the methodology, with "save & return" on every screen.

Tone & trust

Calm, premium, reassuring — privacy stated up front, invitation-only framing, and a warm completion that hands over to the Next Chapter Mentor.
