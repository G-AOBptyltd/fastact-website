Chapter 01

The Learning Path

Our flagship programme is AI Productivity Systems with Claude — a six-module live cohort learning path. It is not a video library you never finish. It runs as a facilitated cohort, so each module is worked through together, applied to your own tools, and defended in front of peers who will notice when a claim does not hold.

The path is deliberately sequenced. You cannot govern what you cannot trust, and you cannot trust what you have not first given proper context. So we begin at the base — Context — and build upward through Trust, Rhythm, System, Connection and finally Product, where the whole thing becomes a repeatable way of working rather than a set of tricks.

The curriculum is practitioner-led training, developed within our R&D program and refined against live delivery on real backlogs — including work with Atlassian and Jira-using teams. It is methodology under active R&D development, revised each cohort as the tools and the craft move.

Chapter 03

The Governance Method

The flagship advisory offering is AI Cognitive Governance & Context Saturation — previously known as the GSD Framework, "the human operating system for the AI age". It treats attention and cognitive load as the scarce resources they actually are, and designs a team's working environment around protecting them.

Five pillars carry the method. Cognitive Load Diagnostics measures where the team is drowning. Attention Architecture redesigns the day around focus. Demand Governance controls what work is allowed in. AI Guardrail Design sets the boundaries within which AI can be trusted to act. And Deep Work Protection defends the conditions under which the best work is actually done.

Context saturation is the connective idea: an AI system is only as useful as the situation it has been given, and a team is only as effective as the context it can hold without breaking. The engagements make both explicit, then govern them deliberately.

Attention is the scarce resource. Governance is how you defend it. — On the method
Chapter 04

Why Practitioners

Most AI training is written by people who have not shipped anything with the tools they teach. Ours is not. The Academy is backed by seventeen-plus years of agile and lean delivery expertise, combined with live AI capability building — and, unusually, by working software.

Agility Ops builds and runs its own products: the InSite analytics suite and a set of Jira apps used inside real delivery teams. That means the training is not theory dressed as practice. When we teach how to wire AI into the tools a team lives in, we are teaching from software we maintain, on the same platform — Atlassian and Jira — that our audience already works in.

The result is depth over commodity. Anyone can resell a generic online course. Very few can teach applied AI from the position of also having to ship it, support it, and stand behind it. That is the difference this manual is written to make legible.

Training grounded in production work, not slideware. If we teach it, we have shipped it. — The differentiator
