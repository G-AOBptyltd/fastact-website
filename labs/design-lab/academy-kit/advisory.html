The framework

Five pillars for the human operating system.

A structured way to design how attention, mental load and AI guardrails work together — so adopting AI relieves cognitive load rather than adding to it. Each pillar is drawn from real delivery work, not repackaged theory.

01

Cognitive Load Diagnostics

We start by measuring where attention and mental load actually go across a team — the meetings, context-switches and hidden work that quietly consume capacity. You get an honest baseline before adding another tool, so investment lands where the constraint really is.

02

Attention Architecture

We design the environment so deep work is the default, not the exception — how the working day is shaped, how tools and notifications are configured, and how teams signal focus. Small structural changes compound into a workplace where sustained thinking is normal.

03

Demand Governance

We help you control the inflow of work and interruptions — who can put what on whom, how requests are triaged, and where the queues sit. Governing demand at the source is the difference between a team that chooses its priorities and one that is perpetually reacting.

04

AI Guardrail Design

We build practical governance for how AI is used inside real workflows — what is allowed, what is reviewed, and how judgement stays with people. Guardrails make AI safe and reviewable in day-to-day work rather than a policy PDF nobody reads.

05

Deep Work Protection

We treat focus time as a first-class asset and protect it deliberately — the demand governance, attention architecture and team norms that keep the focused work AI is meant to amplify from being eroded by the everyday. Protected focus is where the real leverage lives.

How we work

Diagnostic, then design, then embed.

A pragmatic three-phase engagement that leaves capability with your people — not a dependency on us. Every phase is grounded in your real work, not a generic template.

1 · Diagnostic

We map where cognitive load and interruptions actually sit, and where AI is helping or hurting — an evidence-based read of the current operating system before any change is proposed.

2 · Design

Together we design the attention architecture, demand governance and AI guardrails that fit how your team really works — practical, reviewable and sized to what you can adopt.

3 · Embed

We embed the new patterns into everyday delivery and hand over the practices — so protected deep work and safe AI use outlast the engagement and stay owned by your people.

Who it's for

Built for leaders accountable for how work actually gets done.

The advisory practice is designed for enterprise leaders, PMOs, CIOs and steering committees weighing how their teams should adopt AI without drowning people in cognitive load. Methodology under active R&D development, refined in the open on real delivery work.
