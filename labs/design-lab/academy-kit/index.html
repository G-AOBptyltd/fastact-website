The flagship learning path

AI Productivity Systems with Claude.

A six-module live cohort — built for people who do the work, not just talk about it. Each module compounds on the last, from context discipline through to shipping real product with AI.

01

Context

Give AI the right ground truth. Project intelligence, scope and the context that turns a generic assistant into a capable teammate.

02

Trust

Know when to lean in and when to verify. Building judgement, guardrails and honest expectations of what AI can and can't do.

03

Rhythm

Make AI part of how you work, not a novelty. Daily loops, cadence and the habits that keep output flowing.

04

System

Move from one-off prompts to repeatable systems — reusable context, commands and workflows your whole team can run.

05

Connection

Wire AI into the tools you already use. Integrations, connectors and the plumbing that removes repeat manual steps.

06

Product

Ship something real. Take the capability you've built and turn it into delivered work — the capstone of the path.
