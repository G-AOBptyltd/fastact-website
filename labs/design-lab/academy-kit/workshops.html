How workshops run

Live, small, and practical by design.

Every session is run live in a small group so people practise, not just watch. Two delivery models are available now — with a new public track opening on 18 September.

Fully virtual

Live, interactive sessions delivered online — practical for distributed teams, no travel required. Available now.

In-house

Run for your team, on your context, at your site. Shaped around the work your people actually do. Available now.

Public training New

Open-registration public sessions — a new track starting with our first bookable workshop on 18 September.

Who should attend

Anyone doing the work who wants to use AI well — not just talk about it.

Practitioners, team leads, delivery managers and the people around them — no deep technical background required, just a willingness to practise on real work. Methodology under active R&D development, refined in the open.
