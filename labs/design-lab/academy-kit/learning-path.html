The learning path

Six modules that compound.

Each session builds on the one before it. You start by giving AI solid ground truth and finish by shipping delivered work — with the judgement, systems and integrations to keep going after the cohort ends.

01

Context

Stop re-explaining your work every session. We establish the project intelligence — scope, conventions, ground truth and the standing context — that turns a generic assistant into a teammate that already knows the terrain. This is the foundation everything else sits on.

02

Trust

Know when to lean on AI output and when to check it. We calibrate honest expectations of what current models can and can't do, build a habit of verification proportionate to the stakes, and keep judgement firmly with the person doing the work.

03

Rhythm

Make AI part of how you actually work, not an occasional novelty. We build a daily working loop — the build-and-verify cadence — so drafting, checking and refining flow together and the tool earns its place in your day rather than interrupting it.

04

System

Turn ad-hoc prompting into something repeatable. We package what works into reusable context, commands and playbooks — so a good result is a standard you can run again, and hand to the rest of your team, instead of a lucky one-off.

05

Connection

Wire AI into the tools you already live in. We cover integrations, connectors and the workflow plumbing — including MCP — that removes repeat manual steps and lets AI reach across the systems your team uses, safely and on your terms.

06

Product

Ship something real. The capstone takes the capability you've built and points it at a genuine outcome — then measures value delivered, not activity logged. You leave with delivered work and a system for producing more of it.

How the cohort runs

Live sessions, small groups, built from real delivery work.

This is a facilitated live cohort, not a video library. Sessions are run in small groups so there's room to work through your own examples, and the material is drawn from production delivery — methodology under active R&D development, refined in the open. You bring real work; you leave with real systems.

Who it's for

People who do the work.

The path suits teams adopting AI responsibly and the leaders guiding them — practitioners who want capability they can trust, not a demo they can't reproduce.

Teams adopting AI responsibly

Groups moving past scattered experiments who want shared standards, verification habits and repeatable systems — AI adopted with judgement, not hype.

Atlassian & Jira users

Delivery teams working in Jira and connected tooling who want AI wired into their existing workflow — connectors and integrations that fit how they already ship.

Leaders protecting attention

Managers and leads who want AI to relieve cognitive load rather than add to it — designing the guardrails and deep-work protection that keep focus intact.

The outcome

You finish with delivered work, not a folder of prompts.
