What makes a landing page feel expensive?

Three studies of the same product — SprintINSite, predictive sprint analytics for Jira — each rebuilt in the design language of a site we admire. Same content, three philosophies. The goal: extract transferable patterns for the InSite suite, then fold the winners into our shared chrome. Nothing here ships as-is.

Study A · Typeform

Conversational editorial

Huge serif type on warm cream, one idea per screen, cinematic scroll pacing, a one-question-at-a-time interactive demo.

How to read these studies

  • Each page is deliberately self-contained and bespoke — the point is to feel each language at full strength, not to match our current chrome.
  • Judge on: first-impression weight, typographic confidence, scroll rhythm, and whether the product feels premium before you read a word.
  • All pages are noindex and unlinked. Anything we adopt gets rebuilt properly into the shared InSite chrome — no page ships from this lab.
  • Copy rules still apply here: SprintINSite is predictive analytics (not "AI-powered"), Australian English, no unverifiable claims.
