What makes a landing page feel expensive?

Seven studies across three of our products — each page takes one product and rebuilds it in the design language of a site we admire. Same content, radically different philosophies. The goal: extract transferable patterns, then fold the winners into our shared InSite chrome. Nothing here ships as-is.

How to read these studies

  • Each page is deliberately self-contained and bespoke — the point is to feel each language at full strength, not to match our current chrome.
  • Judge on: first-impression weight, typographic confidence, scroll rhythm, and whether the product feels premium before you read a word.
  • All pages are noindex and unlinked. Anything we adopt gets rebuilt properly into the shared chrome for that site — no page ships from this lab.
  • Copy rules still apply here: SprintINSite and SurveyInSite are predictive/survey analytics (not "AI-powered"), the Academy uses in-progress R&D language, Australian English throughout, no unverifiable claims.
