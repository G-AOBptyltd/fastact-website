Questions

Is this AI or machine learning?
No. SprintINSite uses predictive sprint analytics — a transparent velocity calculation over your recent sprints. Every number on screen is one you can reproduce by hand, which is exactly the point.
How many sprints do I need before it's useful?
Even two or three completed sprints give a usable forecast. The more sprints you feed it, the tighter and steadier the confidence band becomes.
Does it change how we plan in Jira?
Not at all. SprintINSite reads your existing velocity and gives you a forward view alongside it. Your boards, workflows and ceremonies stay exactly as they are.
Can I try it without connecting Jira?
Yes. The forecast panel at the top of this page runs entirely in your browser — type in a few velocity figures and it computes live, no account required.
