Features

AU data residency Real-time results Unlimited responses CSV export

Eleven instruments

Ready-made question types.

Pick the instrument the research actually calls for — each one is configured and ready to publish, from a quick poll to a full conjoint preference study.

01 / POLL

Quick Poll

A single multiple-choice question for a fast read on where people stand.

02 / SCALE

Likert Agreement

A five-point agree-to-disagree scale for measuring sentiment on a statement.

03 / RATING

Star / Rating

A star or numeric rating for scoring experience, quality or satisfaction.

04 / OPEN

Open Feedback

A free-text question for capturing comments, ideas and context in people's own words.

05 / PROFILE

Demographic

Structured profile questions so you can segment and compare results by group.

06 / RANK

Ranked Choice

Ask respondents to order a set of options from most to least preferred.

07 / SPEND

Budget Allocation

Distribute a fixed budget across options to reveal real spending trade-offs.

08 / MATRIX

Importance vs Performance

Rate each item on both importance and performance to find priority gaps.

09 / ANNUAL

Annual Satisfaction

A repeatable satisfaction instrument for tracking sentiment year on year.

10 / MAXDIFF

Priority Ranking (MaxDiff)

Best-worst scaling that forces clear trade-offs to rank priorities with confidence.

11 / CONJOINT

Design Option Preference (Conjoint)

Present bundled options so respondents choose between realistic combinations, not features in isolation.

Platform capabilities

Everything runs without limits or lock-in.

The parts you rely on every day — collecting responses, watching results land, and getting your data back out cleanly.

01 / VOLUME

Unlimited responses

Every survey takes unlimited responses. No per-response caps or overage charges.

02 / LIVE

Real-time dashboard

Results update live as people reply, so you can watch a picture form as it happens.

03 / EXPORT

CSV export

Export clean, structured results to CSV at any time for your own analysis.

04 / ACCESS

Anonymous access

Respondents can answer anonymously, lowering the barrier to honest feedback.

05 / ISOLATION

Row-level data isolation

Each survey's responses are kept isolated at the row level so data never bleeds between projects.

06 / SECURITY

Encryption in transit

Responses are encrypted in transit between respondents and the platform.
