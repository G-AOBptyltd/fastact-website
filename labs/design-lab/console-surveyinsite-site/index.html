Why it matters

Your respondents' data stays in Australia.

Everything runs in AWS Sydney with row-level isolation between surveys and encryption in transit. No offshore respondent data, no third-party panel resale — just the answers you collected.

01 / RESIDENCY

AU data residency

Hosted in AWS Sydney. Respondent data never leaves the region.

02 / ISOLATION

Row-level isolation

Each survey's responses are isolated at the row level, with encryption in transit.

03 / ACCESS

Anonymous access

Respondents can answer anonymously. Export clean results to CSV at any time.
