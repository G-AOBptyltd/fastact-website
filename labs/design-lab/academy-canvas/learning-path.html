The six modules

One path, taught in the order the work needs it.

Each module hands you a working capability you use the same week — a technique, not a certificate. Read straight down, or jump to any node above.

Module 01 / Context

Context

Build the project intelligence that stops AI needing re-explaining. You leave with a saturated context — the standing brief, the constraints, the domain — so every session starts warm instead of cold.

Module 02 / Trust

Trust

Calibrate how much to rely on AI output, and learn to verify what matters. You leave able to tell where the model is dependable, where it needs a check, and how to keep your judgement in the loop.

Module 03 / Rhythm

Rhythm

Run the daily build-and-verify loop with AI — the working cadence that turns lucky prompts into reliable delivery. You leave with a repeatable rhythm you can run on real tasks, not a one-off demo.

Module 04 / System

System

Turn ad-hoc prompting into repeatable playbooks. You leave with reusable patterns for the work you do most — captured, named and shareable — so the second time is faster than the first.

Module 05 / Connection

Connection

Integrate AI across your tools and your team — MCP, connectors and the seams between people and systems. You leave able to wire AI into where work actually happens, not a side window.

Module 06 / Product

Product

Ship a real outcome your organisation uses — and measure value, not activity. The capstone: you leave with something in production and a way to prove it moved the work forward.

Who it's for

For people who ship — and want AI to help them ship better.

The path assumes you do real work with real stakes. It suits practitioners and the leaders who back them.

Audience / Individual

Practitioners

Delivery leads, analysts, engineers and PMs who want a dependable way to work with AI on real tasks.

Audience / Team

Whole teams

Teams who want a shared working loop and playbooks, so AI capability is consistent — not one person's trick.

Audience / Leaders

Leaders & PMOs

Leaders responsible for capability and governance who need their people current — with judgement kept in the loop.

Why this order

The path mirrors how real work compounds.

You can't build trust without context, or rhythm without trust. Each module stands on the one before it — so by the capstone you're not learning AI, you're shipping with it.

B01

Context and trust come before technique — get these right and everything after it needs less fixing.
B02

Rhythm and system turn one-off prompting into a cadence and playbooks you actually reuse.
B03

Connection and product push the loop out across your tools and into something your organisation uses.

The methodology is developed within our R&D program and refined against production work.
