Key cross-site findings
Header model is split 3 ways: corporate menu (4 sites) · own-wordmark + Products dropdown (2) · left sidebar (FACT). The documented "by Agility Ops ↗" byline isn't literally implemented anywhere yet.
Type is near-universal: Inter (body) + Plus Jakarta Sans (display) on 6 of 7 — only SprintINSite uses system fonts.
Buttons mostly pill 9999px (5 sites), primary #0f172a → accent hover; SprintINSite (4px) and FACT (10px) diverge.
Footer: full navy footer + canonical product list + LinkedIn on 5 sites; SprintINSite & PortfolioInSite carry no product list. Product order/count/naming drifts (7 / 9 / 10 items; "FACT Training" vs "Agility Ops Academy").
Logos: no image assets anywhere — every site uses a CSS gradient monogram box (AO/PI/RI…) + text wordmark.
Pricing is genuinely split: central (Sprint/Portfolio/Report) vs own on-site Stripe (SurveyInSite, CareerInSite — both live) vs CMS cards (FACT).
