Why teams choose it

Three jobs, done quietly in the background.

Predict

A transparent velocity calculation projects your next sprint's likely range from real completed work — no black box, just maths you can explain to a steering committee.

Detect

Spot the drift early — over-commitment, slipping throughput and unstable cycle time surface before they cost you a release, not after the retro.

Report

Board-ready snapshots in a click. Share a clean forecast and delivery trend with stakeholders who never open Jira — and keep everyone on the same number.
