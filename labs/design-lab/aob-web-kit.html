DELIVERABLE · The rulebook
The standard, in 6 rules

Everything you need to remember.

If a page follows these six rules, it belongs to the kit. That is the entire standard.

1

One kit, every site

All seven products share the same components, tokens and layout primitives. No bespoke chrome.

2

Swap the accent — nothing else

Change a single custom property. The whole site re-skins. Never hand-roll a colour.

3

Product-first header

Product lockup and nav lead. A dimmed "by Agility Ops ↗" byline is the only outbound corporate link.

4

One canonical footer

The same dark footer with the full ten-product list on every site — the cross-suite connective tissue.

5

One type scale + pill buttons

Plus Jakarta for display, Inter for body. Pill buttons only. Consistent radii and spacing rhythm.

6

Warm, light, generous

A warm near-white canvas, hairline borders, real whitespace. Calm and premium by default.
