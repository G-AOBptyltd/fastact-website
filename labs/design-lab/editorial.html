The Programme — a field study

Training built by people who ship, for teams who have to.

Applied AI training is crowded with commodity courses that stop at the demo. Agility Ops Academy takes the opposite stance: every module is grounded in live delivery work and backed by software we build and run ourselves. The result is depth over surface — training that survives contact with a real backlog, a real governance review, and a real Monday morning.

Our flagship is AI Productivity Systems with Claude, a six-module live cohort learning path. It is themed as a progression — Context, Trust, Rhythm, System, Connection, Product — so that capability compounds week over week rather than arriving as disconnected tips. This methodology is under active R&D development within our R&D program, and it is taught by practitioners.

Fig. 01 — Cohort in session, context-saturation lab

A curriculum, not a playlist

Where most online training hands you a video library, the Academy runs as a facilitated cohort. Sessions are live, the work is your work, and the through-line is a system you keep. Alongside the flagship path sit free practitioner guides, standalone workshops, and an advisory practice for teams that need change at the operating-model level.

  • AI Productivity Systems with Claude — the six-module live cohort learning path.
  • Practitioner Guides — free, field-tested references you can use today.
  • Workshops — prompt engineering, AI governance, context-saturation labs, agile fundamentals.
  • Advisory — AI Cognitive Governance & Context Saturation for leadership teams.
  • Claude Playbooks — reusable patterns for teams working inside the Claude ecosystem.
  • Tools & Games — interactive practice for the habits that stick.

Backed by software we run

The differentiator is not a promise, it is a portfolio. Our training is informed by the analytics and Jira apps we build and operate across the InSite suite — so what we teach about measurement, governance and delivery is drawn from systems in production, particularly for Atlassian and Jira-using teams. Illustratively, that means a curriculum that keeps pace with the tools rather than trailing behind them.
