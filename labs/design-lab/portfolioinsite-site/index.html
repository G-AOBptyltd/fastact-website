What it does

Governance you can see and defend.

Six capabilities that turn a scattered backlog of initiatives into a portfolio a steering committee can actually govern.

Portfolio scoring

Rank every initiative on a consistent, weighted model — value, risk, effort and strategic fit — so priorities are transparent and repeatable.

Health score

A single, at-a-glance signal for each initiative, rolled up across the portfolio, so the committee sees where attention is genuinely needed.

Scenario modelling

Test trade-offs before you commit. Add, defer or drop initiatives and watch the mix, capacity and outcomes shift in real time.

Investment mix

See how spend and effort spread across themes, horizons and business units — and rebalance deliberately, not by accident.

Delivery pipeline

Track initiatives from intake to done, sourced live from Jira, so the governance view and the delivery reality never drift apart.

AI-assisted auto-scoring

Draft scores from the evidence already in Jira, with the reasoning shown. AI proposes — your governance model and people decide.

Where it lives

Works with Jira Cloud — no extra data silo.

PortfolioInSite is a Jira Forge app. It reads your projects, epics and initiatives directly, so governance stays anchored to real delivery data. Nothing to export, nothing to keep in sync by hand.
