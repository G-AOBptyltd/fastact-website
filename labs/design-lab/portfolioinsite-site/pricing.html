Questions

The pricing details, plainly.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every plan opens with a 14-day free trial so your committee can prioritise real initiatives before committing. You can start without entering card details.

Which products does this cover?

This pricing covers PortfolioInSite — the Jira Forge governance app and its embedded tools. Other InSite products carry their own plans, priced on their own pages.

How is it billed?

Billing runs on PortfolioInSite's own Stripe catalogue and is processed securely at checkout. The look and feel is shared across the suite; the commerce is specific to each product.

Can I change tiers?

Yes. You can move between tiers as your portfolio grows or contracts — the change applies to your next billing cycle, with no need to start over.
