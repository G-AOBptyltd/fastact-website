What it does

Six capabilities, one governed view.

Each turns a scattered set of initiatives into a portfolio a steering committee can actually see, defend and decide on.

Portfolio scoring

Rank every initiative on a consistent, weighted model — value, risk, effort and strategic fit — so priorities are transparent, repeatable and easy to defend in the room.

Health score

A single, at-a-glance signal for each initiative, rolled up across the portfolio, so the committee sees where attention is genuinely needed — not just where it is loudest.

Scenario modelling

Test trade-offs before you commit. Add, defer or drop initiatives and watch the mix, capacity and outcomes shift, so you decide with the consequences in view.

Investment mix

See how spend and effort spread across themes, horizons and business units — and rebalance deliberately, so the portfolio reflects strategy rather than momentum.

Delivery pipeline

Track initiatives from intake to done, sourced live from Jira, so the governance view and the delivery reality never quietly drift apart between meetings.

AI-assisted auto-scoring

Draft scores from the evidence already in Jira, with the reasoning shown. AI proposes a starting point; your governance model and your people make the call.

How it works

From Jira data to a governed decision, in four steps.

No new data silo, no manual exports — governance sits directly on the delivery data you already keep in Jira.

Step 1

Connect to Jira Cloud

Install the Forge app and PortfolioInSite reads your projects, epics and initiatives in place — nothing to export or re-key.

Step 2

Auto-score initiatives

AI drafts a first-pass score from the evidence in Jira, with its reasoning shown, ready for your team to confirm or adjust.

Step 3

Model scenarios

Compare options side by side — add, defer or drop work and watch the mix, capacity and outcomes respond before you commit.

Step 4

Govern the mix

Lock the decision in a governed view the committee shares — one picture, defensible priorities, no duelling spreadsheets.

Built for Jira Cloud

Where governance and delivery share one source of truth.

PortfolioInSite runs as a Jira Forge app and as a standalone web view for those without a Jira licence. Your data stays in your Atlassian tenant, and role-based views give executives, the PMO and delivery teams the picture each of them needs.
